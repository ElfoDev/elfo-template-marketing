# LinkedIn post

## Files

- `post.md`: the post text (the first two lines decide whether people click "more"; 1,300 characters at most), 0–3 hashtags.
- Optional `image-01.png`: **1200 × 1200**, or a document carousel `slides.pdf` made from 1080 × 1350 slides.

## Rules

- First person, one lesson or one story, concrete numbers only from brand.md.
- Short paragraphs, no emoji bullets, the link in the post (not "in the comments").
