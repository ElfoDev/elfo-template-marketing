# YouTube video (landscape)

## Files

- `post.md`: **title** (under 70 characters, the keyword first), **description** (first two lines carry the point and
  the link; then chapters with timestamps), **tags** (5–15), the playlist if any.
- `video.mp4`: **1920 × 1080**, 30 fps, H.264. 30 seconds to 3 minutes for a product video.
- `thumbnail.png`: **1280 × 720**, under 2 MB: 3–5 big words, high contrast, one visual, readable as a small tile.

## Rules

- The first 5 seconds say what the viewer gets. No logo intro.
- Every scene has on-screen text that carries the message with the sound off (most people watch muted at first).
- End with one call to action and the link (also in the description).
- With voice-over (`ELEVENLABS_API_KEY`), the on-screen text follows the narration; without it, captions carry everything.
