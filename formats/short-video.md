# Short vertical video (Reels, Shorts, TikTok)

## Files

- `post.md`: caption per platform (Instagram, YouTube Shorts title, TikTok), hashtags (3–6).
- `video.mp4`: **1080 × 1920**, 30 fps, H.264, **15–45 seconds**.
- `cover.png`: 1080 × 1920, the frame people see in the grid.

## Rules

- The hook is on screen in the first second. Cut every 2–4 seconds.
- Keep text inside the safe area: 120 px from the top, 320 px from the bottom, 60 px from the sides (platform buttons
  and captions cover the rest).
- Big captions (56 px or larger), 3–6 words at a time.
