# Instagram carousel

## Files

- `post.md`: caption (first line is the hook, under 125 characters before "more"), then the body, then 3–8 hashtags,
  and alt text for each slide.
- `slide-01.png` … `slide-10.png`: **1080 × 1350** (4:5), 3 to 10 slides.

## Rules

- Slide 1 is the hook: one bold sentence, readable in a second. The last slide is the call to action.
- One idea per slide, 25 words at most, text 44 px or larger, generous margins (90 px; Instagram crops the grid to 3:4).
- Same layout across slides so the carousel reads as one piece; slide numbers help ("2/7").
